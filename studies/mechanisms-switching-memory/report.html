What can make a system switch and remember?

NousVolition / My-Sources-Project-ALL · completed computational supplement · 11 October 2026

A spring can remember which side a bead reached. A feedback reaction model can remember a brief stimulus. Both effects appear in these completed calculations, but their causes and the quantities being measured differ.

18 verification tests passed; 168 seeded jump simulations ran. The spring force follows the supplied textbook geometry. The chemical model is an explicit, uncalibrated hypothesis with chosen rates. It is not a fitted model of the photographed experiment, and it does not assign new reactions to CO, CO₂, water, VOCs, smoke or H₂O₂.

1. A defined physical reason for outward motion

The screenshot gives the equilibrium of a bead on a tilted wire, attached to an ideal spring whose length is √(x²+a²). Its relaxed length is L₀. Taking positive x downhill gives:

F(x)=mg sinθ − kx[1−L₀/√(x²+a²)]
V(x)=½k[√(x²+a²)−L₀]²−mgx sinθ

This ideal spring can push when compressed. At zero tilt and L₀>a, it pushes a slightly displaced bead away from the center. It stops doing so when the geometry lets the spring reach its relaxed length. The two stable resting positions are ±√(L₀²−a²). This outward motion is an elastic force on one bead; it does not establish repulsion between molecules or droplets.

For the chosen a=10 mm, L₀=20 mm and k=1 N/m, the resting positions are ±17.32051 mm. Mass is 10 g and linear drag is γ=2 N·s/m. The implemented overdamped equation is γẋ=F(x), with time unit γ/k=2 s. The neglected inertia ratio mk/γ²=0.0025; this selects a strong-drag approximation, not a measured apparatus. No thermal forcing, dry friction, spring buckling, tension-only string or geometric contact is included.

Writing y=x/a, λ=L₀/a and b=mg sinθ/(ka), the dynamics become y′=b−y+λy/√(1+y²). For λ>1, the static folds occur at |b|=(λ2/3−1)3/2. Here that is 0.45019646, or a tilt of ±2.6312°. Faster ramps switch beyond the static fold because the bead needs time to move. The recorded switch is its crossing of x=0; that is distinct from disappearance of a static equilibrium.

2. A chemical hypothesis that can be rejected or refined

Let p be the fraction of a fixed protein population in an active, phosphorylated state. We chose a bounded cooperative activation model:

dp/dt = [s(t)+v pⁿ/(Kⁿ+pⁿ)](1−p) − d p
v=2 s⁻¹, K=0.3, n=4, d=1 s⁻¹; total protein=1 µM

The activation term uses inactive protein (1−p); deactivation restores it. The interval 0≤p≤1 is invariant. These parameters are assumptions selected to test bistability, not measured enzyme constants. The Hill exponent represents an effective cooperative response, not proof of a four-molecule collision or an identified catalyst. It differs deliberately from any incomplete equation below the screenshot crop.

At zero stimulus, the active fractions are 0, 0.17662103 and 0.65715263. The middle state is unstable; the other two are stable in this deterministic model. A pulse s=0.5 s⁻¹ starting from p=0 needs to last longer than 0.43903245 s to cross that boundary. The times are consequences of our assumed rates.

Under sustained feedback and replenished ATP, the longer pulse leaves about 65.715% active. With no feedback, the response decays. Stopping the ATP-dependent activation flux after the pulse also makes activity decay. At the high steady state, activation and deactivation remain nonzero and equal: about 0.657153 µM/s each. A constant concentration therefore does not mean that chemical events have stopped.

Bookkeeping assumes P+ATP→P*+ADP and P*+H₂O→P+Pi, with buffered proton/water reservoirs and unspecified effective enzymes. P+P*=1 µM is fixed. The replenished-fuel case records ATP consumption and phosphate release, checking P*(t)−P*(0)=activations−deactivations. It does not claim complete atom/charge or heat closure for a specified enzyme chemistry. No chemical potentials or calorimetry were supplied, so this is not a thermodynamic validation.

A separate finite-fuel test starts in the high state with 2 µM ATP, no replenishment, and an assumed ATP availability multiplier ATP/(0.1 µM+ATP). It falls to p=6.8e-17 after 40 s. Adenylate ATP+ADP and the phosphate count 3ATP+2ADP+Pi+P* are conserved to 7.11e-15 µM. Its particular depletion time depends on this additional assumed law; it is not a measurement.

3. What connects the three screenshots

The refuge/outbreak drawing illustrates two stable states separated by an unstable branch. The supplied crop does not contain its full population equation, so no population calculation is claimed. The spring calculation independently produces the same kind of folded equilibrium surface. Near y=0, its rate is b+(λ−1)y−(λ/2)y³+O(y⁵): changing compression and bias supplies two controls for the familiar imperfect bifurcation. This is a mathematical relationship between model structures.

The phosphorylation screenshot proposes a specific feedback mechanism. Xiong and Ferrell (2003) reported maintained kinase activity after transient stimulation in Xenopus oocytes and loss of that persistence when feedback was blocked. That supplies biological evidence for the mechanism in that system, not the numerical constants or single-variable equation used here. The paper has a 2007 corrigendum; no experimental values or figures from it were fitted. Cooperative activation/deactivation models also appear in Smolen, Baxter and Byrne (2009); our single-loop construction is not a reproduction of their full network.

Thus the models can share a computational test suite and concepts such as stability, thresholds and hysteresis. Their variables remain bead position, population level and active protein fraction. No interaction between these systems has been assumed.

At zero stimulus, p=0 is absorbing in the finite-count model: eventually a finite stochastic system can lose its active population. These finite runs do not determine that eventual escape time. A nonzero background activation rate would change this assumption. Sensitivity calculations vary v, K, n and d one at a time; weaker feedback or altered cooperativity can remove bistability altogether. Geometry sensitivity similarly removes the spring's two resting positions when L₀≤a. Full roots and conditional thresholds are saved in results.json; this is scenario sensitivity, not uncertainty estimated from measured parameters.

What this adds to the substance research

You can propose a mechanism first and test its consequences. These completed examples give concrete predictions: two bead positions, finite switching thresholds, persistent activity with ongoing fuel use, and loss of activity when fuel is limited. To apply such a hypothesis to one of the original substance pairs, specify the relevant force or reaction network and obtain or estimate its parameters. A useful hypothesis makes a prediction that controls or observations could disprove.

The existing dissolution, transport and charged-droplet results retain their own models. Nothing here shows that CO mixed with water undergoes phosphorylation, that neutral droplets repel, or that a new Navier–Stokes law is required. The numerical findings are complete for the stated cases; experimental validation of the proposed biochemical model remains open.
