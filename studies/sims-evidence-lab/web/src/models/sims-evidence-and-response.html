    Decision rules
    Copy: random neighbor, 15% refusal of a different choice; optional 2% flip. Hold: original starting choice. Majority / oppose: local majority / opposite; retain current choice on ties.
    Evidence
    Predictions recorded before actions; likelihoods scored on observed context and outcomes. Four rules, each starting at 25% support. After each action, multiply each weight by its predicted probability of that action, then normalize the four weights to total 100%. Actual rule settings excluded from investigator scoring.
    Response trigger
    At least 12 actions; a unique alternative with ≥80% relative support and ≥10× the stated rule’s likelihood. Below threshold: retain settings and request more evidence.
    Responses
    Editable, deterministic participant policies. Dialogue is generated from evidence and policy; the investigator is named Feynman in this model.
    Sequence
    Collect evidence → show each SIM its own record → apply replies → collect fresh actions. Decisions pause during the nine exchanges.
    Follow-up
    Fresh likelihoods start from equal weights. Network: 120 seconds, 25% activation, shuffled turns. Probes: own choice and neighbor choices fixed; 8 repetitions per distinct context.
    Behavior change
    The table shows actual rule changes inside the simulator. Fresh-action evidence independently reports which rule the observations support.
