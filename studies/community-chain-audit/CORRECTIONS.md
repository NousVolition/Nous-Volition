## Exact correction to the Miro alternative-law prototype

[Model choices, item 3458764687920695509](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVEXBJz3k=/?moveToWidget=3458764687920695509) is a proposal, not the corpus law. For a finite pulse followed by constant A=A∞ and added relaxation −μS, the total obeys M′=κA∞−μM, hence M∞=κA∞/μ, not zero unless the source vanishes. With γ=κ=μ=1 the check gives A(40)=M(40)=1.

For the separate proposal A′=γΔU−λA, M′=κA, zero initial A/M and finite-duration input, integration gives M∞=(κγ/λ)∫ΔU dt. The +1/−1 and reversed balanced pulses both tend to zero, although their finite-time trajectories differ. The check at time 40 gives M=0 to floating precision in both orders. These alternatives are not added to the exact corpus PDE.

The AI comparison board also uses an explicitly different odd exponential extension for negative q in later arithmetic prompts. That benchmark definition is not the corpus's one-sided exact-off law. Correct answers to that different question do not verify the corpus force.
