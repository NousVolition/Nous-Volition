What still needs testing

The full empirical two-in-one has not been validated. The remaining work is an identified observation model connecting real inward putting-forward, receiving, the moving idea's forms and each person's history to predicted quantities. Partial observations can be sufficient if latent states and parameters are identifiable with uncertainty; every field value need not be directly observed. Calibration, prehistory, measurement error, intended-meaning review and untouched future outcomes must be specified. See measurement requirements.

Physical/soil measurements, external conversations, hypothetical allocation policies and philosophical images can inform questions and tests. They do not automatically measure inward Smoke, Clay or a moving idea. Strong language is retained when the account supports it; missing evidence is stated precisely rather than weakening the author's terms.
