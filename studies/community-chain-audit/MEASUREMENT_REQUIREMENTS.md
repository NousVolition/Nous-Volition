# What still needs a real test

Smoke, Clay and the moving idea remain within one person's two-in-one. Preserve the person's own wording, whether speaking/receiving can be distinguished, and whether the idea is nonverbal. Preserve each person's recorded events, remembered account and present retrieval separately. A label attached to a numerical input is provenance, not an independently evolved Smoke or Clay state.

The full corpus can be tested with partial observations; every continuum value need not be directly observed. Before claiming such a test, specify:

1. The source version and all governing equations, domain, boundary conditions, units, parameters and explicit closures. The separate W, exact-margin and reciprocal relations need their own observed quantities and justified coupling if included in an inward application.
2. Observation operators connecting the actual recorded putting-forward, receiving/reconsideration and changing idea to predicted outputs. Record measurement error, missingness, coding disagreements and what would contradict each operator. Intent, reference, wording and category equality are different observations.
3. Each person's pre-observation state or an identified uncertainty distribution over it. Do not reset missing biography, utility or gaps to zero. Separate memory contents, clarity, accuracy, clock age and felt distance. No monotonic fading is assumed.
4. Evidence distinguishing Water/perceived balance from calm or suppressed disturbance; Fog/clarity from certainty; Wind/felt time from clock time; Mist's exact intended role from the provisional agreement interpretation; D2O's specified small change from a demonstrated consequence. H2O2 remains a separate concept without a settled inward measurement definition.
5. Latent-state/parameter identification and uncertainty. In particular gamma/kappa rescaling and S/threshold/force rescaling can leave observations unchanged. A chosen numerical coordinate or kernel is not a measured mental location.
6. Freeze the mapping, fitting rules and outcome score before reserved future observations. Earlier data that shaped a choice remain development data. Report every failure, eligible denominator, actual integration step and numerical refinement. Controls assess mechanisms; they do not replace the full model.

The available DES episodes, repeated probes, questionnaires and fluency records each supply some observations. None presently supplies or identifies the entire linked measurement model above. That is the reason no full empirical two-in-one score is reported here. It is not a conclusion that the theory is false or that the omitted materials are irrelevant.
