# Connections examined instead of joined merely to complete the model

Some links are the author's definitions, some are useful analogies, and some are explicit experimental hypotheses. They can stay when their status is stated. A failure occurs when a link silently changes category—for example, when an analogy is presented as a measured mechanism, a chosen coordinate as an observation, or a conditional simulation as validation of the whole theory.

The [ledger](connection_ledger.csv) records 33 named connections, a source anchor and snapshot hash for each, what follows, what does not follow, downstream consequences, and the missing test. This is a review of the accessible chain and the named links, not proof that every possible logical error has been eliminated. A source may already state the limitation: such rows are retained as correctly qualified connections, not falsely described as newly discovered misconduct. No motive such as “wanting closure” is inferred.

The clearest newly repaired overreach was **Mist**. The author named a moving-idea form; an earlier interpretation narrowed it to internalized/unspoken agreement. A provisional note was present, but current tables and JSON still stated that narrower definition categorically. The tables, measurement ledger and report generators are now consistent. Frozen protocols and the older editable document preserve the historical proposal, with current notices that supersede it. No saved numerical results were rewritten.

The **shared S/u field** is another important boundary. The momentum and scalar/source components occur in separate corpus sections. Identifying their S and u on one periodic numerical domain is a declared coupling choice. The Miro return path is proposed. The solver retains the exact chosen component laws, but that fact alone does not establish the unique empirical assembly of the whole corpus.

The prior **zero-history resets**, **correctness/reference-to-field mappings**, and **DES spatial/readout choices** remain conditional assumptions. They were not recovered measurements of the person's full history, meaning or inward field. Architecture anchors for transmit and receive are restored without pretending the static coordinates determine role dynamics. The latest workbook analysis was rerun independently of previous predictions, so those numerical predictions are not an input to its scores; its interpretation still needs the missing observation links.

## Dependency consequence

The conceptual path is source → interpretation → observation mapping → initialized numerical model → comparison with observations. A verified last calculation cannot repair an unsupported earlier arrow. Correcting an earlier arrow requires checking its descendants; it does not require discarding unrelated empirical arithmetic. That is why the old conditional scores are preserved, current attribution is corrected, and the new exact-equation numerical checks are not advertised as a full human validation.

The role/form/history labels currently remain metadata. No separate role-specific dynamics, form decoder or full biography is created by attaching those labels. A valid latent-state test need not directly measure every field value, but it must identify the relevant states/parameters with uncertainty and rule out materially different hidden histories that fit the same observations yet predict different outcomes.
