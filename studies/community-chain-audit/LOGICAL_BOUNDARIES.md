# Open connections must remain open

The audit found real risks of premature closure. It establishes observable implementation and reporting choices; it cannot establish an internal motive such as wanting the theory to be closed. Completing an audit does not complete the scientific theory or its empirical test.

| Step in the argument | What is established | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| Corpus equations to numerical fields | Every term of the specified momentum, scalar, accumulator and stress subsystem is retained in a declared periodic-domain approximation. | That this is already a fully specified empirical two-in-one, or that the separate W, utility-margin and reciprocal relations have an established dynamic coupling. |
| Transmit/receive architecture to Smoke/Clay | The corpus contains transmit/I and receive/Self anchors; the author later locates the two-in-one within a person. | That either role has been independently measured, that the original external-input architecture is identical to the later interpretation, or that changing a role label changes the dynamics. |
| Scalar accumulator to personal history | A integrates the declared utility deviation; continuation can preserve a nonzero A and full field state. | That this scalar contains a person's biography, remembered content, accuracy, clarity or felt age. Equal accumulated utility does not imply equal histories or equal experiences. |
| Moving-idea forms to field behavior | The author's forms and clarifications are preserved; provisional elaborations are identified. | That calm is measured Water, that fog-like language measures Fog, or that choosing a fluid coefficient establishes a psychological mechanism. |
| Real records to meaning | Some records include reported inner experience, ordered responses and coder categories. | That category identity establishes intended-meaning identity, that outside conversation is automatically inward dialogue, or that missing observations are zero. |
| Reproducible computation to empirical support | Saved numbers can be reproduced; analytic identities and numerical refinement can be checked. | That those numbers validate the chosen measurement mapping, identify all parameters, or establish causation. |
| Repaired sources to a complete audit | Named errors, dependencies, accessible sources and available checks are documented. | That inaccessible source content has been inspected, every external study re-audited, or every possible error eliminated. |

## Closure assumptions remain assumptions

The component momentum and scalar-source equations occur in different corpus sections. Identifying their S and u on a shared domain is an explicit coupling choice, not something established simply by transcribing both correctly. Miro's proposed return path motivates that choice but does not empirically validate it. The [33-connection review](CONNECTION_AUDIT.md) gives sources, consequences and missing tests for this and the other named conceptual links.

Periodicity, constant coefficients, a chosen kernel, synthetic initial fields and piecewise-constant inputs make numerical fixtures executable. They are disclosed experimental conditions, not conclusions about a mind. Requiring an initial-history provenance string prevents an undocumented reset; it cannot authenticate that history. No new clinical conclusion or diagnosis is drawn from the conceptual forms.

The role, form and personal-history metadata do not currently close the observation-to-state connection. The program must not fill that gap with an invented force, a convenient category score or a simpler predictor and then call the result the author's full model. Retaining a gap is scientifically different from rejecting the idea that the gap may be bridgeable.

## What this release can finish

This release can finish the documented source audit, correct identified inheritance errors, verify the specified numerical subsystem, reproduce available measurements and publish the evidence. The empirical full two-in-one remains an open research requirement. Its next test requires an identified observation model, uncertainty over unobserved history, and outcomes reserved before model choices.
