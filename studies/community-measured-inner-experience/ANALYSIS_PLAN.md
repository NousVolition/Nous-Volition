Recorded 2026-10-23 before computing the new summary statistics. This is an exploratory measurement audit and reproduction, not a preregistration or untouched model-selection holdout: the articles' abstracts, methods and selected published results have already been read. Workbook schemas and sample counts have been inspected.

## Question and boundary

Can the newly downloaded public observations identify the corpus's full coupled field model for one person's inward Smoke, Clay, moving idea and individual history? Preserve the exact corpus equations and the Miro distinction between source equations and proposed operational connections. No simpler predictive model will be fitted. ## Sources and planned checks

1. Moffatt et al. (2020), OSF HJ7TZ, RUM_master_data.xlsx: pair rumination and distraction by participant ID. Report all five supplied VISQ subscale contrasts, not just favorable results. Use complete supplied pairs; retain missing cells. Report source imputation separately, because the paper says two missing distraction items were mean-imputed before aggregation. Flag fractional dialogic/condensed sums as potential source-imputation indicators, not authenticated raw-item provenance. Repeat after excluding the union of those flagged rows. For each contrast report mean in each condition, mean/median difference, positive/tied/negative counts, a paired t interval and a participant bootstrap percentile interval (20,000 resamples; seed 20261023). Add Holm-adjusted paired-test p values across five subscales. Arithmetic on these ordinal sum scores is a descriptive convention. Also report contrasts separately by recorded induction order; these do not identify lifetime history or causal carryover.

Recompute the four-item state mean from supplied numeric values and compare with its saved column. Check survey response recoding against displayed choices; do not silently reverse scores. Inventory ordered response categories and expression records without equating categories with intended meaning. No originality regression or simpler forecast is fitted.
3. Reconcile the corrected DOCX equations with the later PDF board export and live Miro frames. Record exact source hashes, inspected page/frame scope, and unresolved observation links. The board's synthetic examples do not become personal histories.

## Full-model eligibility

Allow a full empirical run only after an explicit observation model links measurements to states/functions, defines a shared domain/metric and time unit, and demonstrates distinguishability of the proposed latent states/parameters. Initial fields and pre-observation accumulation must be measured or estimated with justified uncertainty; unknown history cannot be reset to zero. The exact model also needs an identified utility input/baseline and response law, boundary/source specification and an evaluable future outcome. Not every variable must be directly measured; identifiable latent-state estimation is permissible. None of these missing specifications may be silently invented.
