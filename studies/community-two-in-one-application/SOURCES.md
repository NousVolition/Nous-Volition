## New public records actually downloaded

Kucyi, A., Shareef-Trudeau, L., Braun, D., Peng, H., Bounyarith, T., and Li, J. Z. *A multi-session simultaneous EEG-fMRI dataset with online experience sampling*. [NEMAR DOI](https://doi.org/10.82901/nemar.on007216); [author dataset description](https://github.com/nemarDatasets/on007216/blob/4ff088a521a89176a1b8cd361d145e95f2db1506/dataset_description.json); [README](https://github.com/nemarDatasets/on007216/blob/4ff088a521a89176a1b8cd361d145e95f2db1506/README.md). The metadata declares CC0 and OpenNeuro version 1.0.0.

Pinned repository revision: `4ff088a521a89176a1b8cd361d145e95f2db1506`. Downloaded only 140 functional experience-sampling TSVs and 140 paired JSON dictionaries, 1,593,917 bytes. Every selected file matched its upstream Git blob SHA1. The publication includes file paths/hashes, codebook and coverage results; participant response files remain in the local work directory and can be fetched reproducibly. No EEG, fMRI, clinical questionnaires or personal narratives were downloaded for this analysis.

The dictionary contains verbalness, visualness, attention, movement, affect, arousal, deliberate thought, difficulty disengaging, past/future focus, self/other focus, and confidence. Their actual anchor directions are retained. In particular verbalness and visualness have the reverse orientation from an intuitive 'more means more' reading. Thirteen questions are presented sequentially within each probe; their timestamps are question presentation times, not a continuous recording of the thought itself. Do not treat each question timestamp as a new thought trajectory point.

Eight participant IDs were reserved before response inspection. The audit parses their files for item and timing coverage but does not analyze, summarize or fit their response values. Development records contain 19 nonnumeric/nonfinite response entries; they are not zero-filled. Complete item rows do not imply complete response values.

## Further sources screened

Its paper and availability statement were screened; its participant files were not downloaded. Those dimensions still do not jointly measure the author's roles, forms and autobiographical contents.

Its detailed narratives and imaging records require controlled access; the public availability statement does not authorize inventing those observations or contacting participants.

The earlier [think-aloud data review](../community-two-in-one-field/SOURCES.md) and [questionnaire study](../community-two-in-one/README.md) remain separate sources. Their subjects are not merged into the new 24-person cohort or treated as independent measurements of the same personal history. SciSpace was used for discovery; primary-source dictionaries and availability statements determine measurement coverage.

## Chemical analogy

D2O contains two deuterium atoms and one oxygen atom, whereas H2O2 is hydrogen peroxide. Isotopic substitution and biological consequences require their own conditions; the material name alone does not establish harm. [Berry et al., activity labeling using heavy water](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1420406112) is a primary example of condition-dependent biological use. The current mathematical sensitivity trials do not simulate isotope chemistry, exposure or toxicity.
