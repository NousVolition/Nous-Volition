# Records needed for a full two in one test

The existing public source supplies repeated reports of thought qualities. It does not supply this complete record. The following is a proposed collection and annotation specification, not observations that have already been collected.

Use a person key and a session/episode key. Preserve the person's own wording and allow nonverbal thought, uncertainty and no identifiable inward exchange. Do not force every episode into a material form. Record a form only with its source: participant attribution, independent coding, or model prediction. These are different evidence types.

| Record | What to retain | Comparison it permits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inward putting-forward | Exact account, intended meaning, time, associated remembered events | Whether the proposed Smoke observation follows the person's own report |
| Inward receiving | What was received, held, challenged, refused or reformulated, and when | Whether Clay adds something distinguishable from another utterance |
| Moving idea before and after | Claim, target, force, implications and person-judged meaning retention | Distinguish changed wording, changed meaning and changed reception |
| Personal history references | Event identifiers, known event dates or uncertain ages, the remembered account and present retrieval | Preserve the person's sequence and distinguish an event from later reinterpretation |
| Role-specific history | Which recalled events inform putting-forward and receiving; shared or unknown references allowed | Test whether role assignment changes predictions beyond the identical aggregate input |
| Water | Perceived balance, calm, agreement and unresolved disturbance recorded separately | Test apparent balance that coexists with disturbance |
| Fog | Content, vividness/clarity, certainty and accuracy where independently checkable | Distinguish a vivid old memory from a vague recent one without imposing age-based fading |
| Wind | Actual elapsed time/event age, felt temporal distance and uncertainty | Test felt time separately from clock time or past/future orientation |
| Mist | What is taken for granted, whether endorsed, unresolved disagreement and ability to reconsider | Distinguish unspoken agreement from imposed or merely presumed agreement |
| D2O | Exactly what changed, a declared measure of change size, comparable context and observed consequences | Test small differences with large effects, including helpful or harmful effects under specified criteria |
| Utility | What matters to the person, a defined rating/choice procedure, baseline and units | Calibrate U and gamma rather than rename affect, confidence or attention |
| Field observation | Meaning-space coordinates, distance metric and how reports constrain S and u | Test the continuum hypothesis and distinguish its spatial mechanisms |

History need not be complete to fit a latent model, but unobserved history must be represented by explicit uncertainty and tested for its effect on predictions. A single unknown biography must not be replaced by zero. Distinct remembered events must remain in a ledger even if the field model summarizes some of their influence into A.

Before prediction, fix the observation functions, state/parameter scales, boundary conditions, uncertainty model, and criteria for meaningful improvement. Use early observations to infer a person's state and later reserved observations to evaluate it. Retain people reserved from model selection. Report where the full field adds predictive information over its own no-force and no-source controls. A successful movement prediction alone cannot establish all the other rows.

No participant recruitment, intervention, exposure experiment or contact with researchers was performed. Using future participant records requires appropriate consent and data handling; the current public records are analyzed under their stated license.
