# Why the complete empirical fit cannot yet be performed

The new [primary-record search and analysis](primary_records/README.md) adds actual inward-experience transcripts: 39 indexed samples, 37 after the source's training day, and five closely reviewed episode sections. A separate workbook contains 66 questionnaire records from 23 people. These are more informative about inward functions and remembered content than movement ratings alone. They still do not specify the corpus-to-observation functions or jointly measure all the idea forms. The search result is partial empirical support for investigating the constructs, with no full-model prediction score. Missing states could be inferred in a latent model, but only after an observation and uncertainty model is declared.

The 23 October [corrected corpus and live Miro reconciliation](MIRO_CONNECTIONS.md) has now been completed for the available source and selected board areas. It adds exact-equation tests and source traceability, not the missing participant observations. The shared S–u feedback is explicitly a coupling choice; the board's dashed return path does not establish a measured inward geometry. The board's proposed decay alternatives are not silently substituted for the corpus accumulator.

The full field dynamics can be executed, but the supplied corpus does not define a complete two-in-one observation model. The author requests the exact equations and no substitute predictor. A model that predicts only the newly available movement rating would leave out inward receiving, meaning, personal memory contents and idea forms. It would not satisfy that request.

The original field equations are retained. The author has now defined D2O as the sensitivity question of a small compositional change with potentially large consequences. The complete register of currently supplied meanings is in form_definitions.json. That definition supplies a comparison to investigate, not a benefit/harm classifier.

## What is present and what is absent

The NEMAR on007216 public records contain person, session, run and probe identifiers, question times and self-reports for 13 dimensions. They can establish a record of earlier observed reports for each person. They do not supply lifetime history, the content received inwardly, a utility scale, intended-meaning judgements, or separate observations of the author's forms. Not observing a construct is different from observing that it is absent.

Verbalness does not separate inward speaking from receiving. Confidence in questionnaire answers is not memory clarity. Positive affect is not perceived balance. Past/future orientation is not felt temporal distance. Difficulty disengaging is not a measured memory store. No column is assigned these new meanings.

The first eight participant IDs under the frozen hash order remain reserved. Their response values are not used for model selection or summarized by this audit. File, item and timing coverage are inspected. No empirical prediction score is reported for the full two-in-one.

## Choices that cannot be concealed as exact corpus equations

The corpus supplies S, u, pressure, accumulated A, source J, stress and threshold laws. It does not supply separate evolution equations for Smoke and Clay, contents of recalled events, felt time, perceived balance or form changes. A single field can still support multiple measured outputs, but those observation functions must be defined and calibrated. Adding arbitrary names to field components would not create those measurements.

Completing a testable application requires a continuous domain and metric; a meaning for a unit of S and u; a measured or explicitly latent utility input; an observation-start state consistent with known and unknown prehistory; and observations that distinguish the inward functions and forms. Additional state equations or readouts would be new assumptions, even if every supplied corpus equation remains intact.

The numerical counterexamples in this package test what the exact equations retain about history and what they cannot identify. They use declared synthetic inputs. The real-record analysis tests measurement coverage. These are separate results, neither of which is renamed a full empirical validation.
