# Primary inner-experience records: screening and analysis plan

Saved 23 October 2026 before downloading the primary transcript PDFs. This is a prospective local analysis plan, not a registered protocol or an untouched model-selection holdout. The source paper, dataset-card examples and some indexed descriptions have already been inspected. Candidate choice is informed by those descriptions.

The full corpus equations and the author's current inward interpretation remain the target. The exact corpus and Miro source register are in MIRO_CONNECTIONS.md. No simpler predictor, sentiment-as-utility conversion, automatic material-form classifier or new forgetting law will be substituted.

## Primary questions

1. Does a public source preserve actual same-person experience content, distinguish inward expression from receiving/reconsidering, and retain episode identity and personal-history references?
2. Does it provide a time axis for the sampled experience rather than merely timestamps of the later interview?
3. Can it constrain an explicit observation model for the unchanged coupled equations, including the moving idea's forms, initial-history uncertainty and spatial field structure? Missing directly observed states alone do not disqualify a latent model. An unspecified observation relationship does prevent a meaningful fit.

## Primary-record audit

Inspect the original Lena DES index, its study summary and complete available interview transcripts. The published study counts days 2 through 11, excluding day 1 as method training; retain day 1 separately. Publish source URLs, hashes, page locators and concise analytic codes, not wholesale personal narratives or copyrighted transcripts. Record what was reviewed versus merely downloaded.

For each analyzed sample, distinguish inward speaking, inward hearing, external speech, imagery and possible multiple perspectives. Hearing is not automatically receiving or reconsidering an idea; multiple perspectives do not automatically establish Smoke/Clay. Preserve ambiguity. Record history content, retrospective certainty and temporal information only when stated. Do not infer absence of a phenomenon from absence of measurement.

Annotation is one assistant's exploratory coding checked against source text. It is not independent double coding, participant-verified interpretation or a population prevalence estimate. Source-author descriptions and our conceptual mappings must remain separate. Any quantities reported must be recomputable from the released codes. No causal effect or test accuracy will be calculated from selected examples.

## Eligibility for a full predictive test

A fit needs declared observations y_i(t), observation functions H_i(S,u,A,history), noise/uncertainty, person-specific history treatment, a domain/metric, units/scales, utility inputs or a specified inference model for missing inputs, parameters and boundary conditions. State how Smoke/Clay and the forms are distinguished in observations. Test identifiability using the exact corpus symmetries already checked. Fix choices on development records, then predict genuinely reserved times/people. A holdout can test a specified model; it cannot define its missing variables after the fact.

If these conditions cannot be supplied from the sources, report empirical construct evidence and the exact missing model-to-observation connections. Do not generate a numerical full-model score anyway. Partial records may still constrain future tests, but records from different people or studies must not be joined into a fictitious complete personal history.
