# Real inner-experience records for the full two-in-one

Streams and Rocks · 23 October 2026

**New public records provide concrete evidence about inward speaking, hearing, reconsideration, changing imagery and personal-history references. They do not yet support a completed predictive test of the full two-in-one.** The exact corpus solver is unchanged. No simpler predictor was fitted, no numerical utility was invented, and no material-form labels were assigned to participants.

The new work is an empirical source and construct audit: **22 original files verified, 39 DES samples indexed from one person, 37 after excluding the source's first training day, five selected episodes checked throughout their transcript sections, and a separate workbook with 66 questionnaire records from 23 people.** This is more specific evidence than thought-movement ratings alone. There is no full-model prediction score, and these selected episodes are not a holdout or an estimate of prevalence.

These five cases were chosen because they are relevant, after reading source descriptions. Their annotations are exploratory, made by one assistant; no independent second-coder agreement or participant confirmation of the proposed Smoke/Clay/form mappings is claimed. The [annotation ledger](annotations.json) preserves the qualification for each case. The source's 37 post-training episodes are not all being claimed as fully read or classified: every transcript was indexed, the website descriptions were inspected, and only the five listed episode sections received complete close reading. Three relevant PDF pages were also visually checked to verify participant text versus colored researcher commentary.

The evidence argues against collapsing the model into a single verbalness score: inner hearing, articulation, meaning search, recollection and reconsideration are distinguishable in the accounts. It does **not** determine that all these processes follow the corpus PDEs. It also does not require a literal internal voice; some accounts concern imagery or nonverbal evaluation.

## Source checks that change how the data may be used

* The 11 sampling-day transcripts contain **39 unique sample-start markers**. Two are on training day 1; 37 are on days 2–11. Markers, source pages and interview offsets are in [results.json](results.json). Uninterviewed/skipped samples are not silently counted as observed episodes.
* In sample 9.2 the transcript explicitly qualifies a duration described as milliseconds as nonliteral. It cannot supply numerical time steps for the equations.
* The paper's prose cross-references interchange the interview time ranges for samples 8.3 and 8.5. The original transcript places 8.3 at approximately 13:41 and 8.5 at 35:12. Our record uses the transcript marker and page, not the erroneous secondary locator. This corrects provenance, not the participant's account.
* The separate [inner-speech-in-daily-life study](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1335425) provides a public [workbook and code](https://osf.io/b6vxk/). Its keyed wide sheet has 23 people; the full questionnaire sheet has 23, 22 and 21 records at its three waves, totaling 66. These are repeated category reports, not full thought transcripts or role-specific histories. The study documents an omitted listening item in its paper form; unavailable responses must not be treated as observed absence. No listening rate or clinical association was calculated here.
* The [Mempathy repository](https://osf.io/9z2uf/) has memory-retrieval/empathy recordings, with its descriptions identifying 28 included participants in each of two experiments. We inspected inventories and data descriptions, not raw EEG/fMRI. Those descriptions do not establish the needed inward-role and idea-form observations. This is a limited screening finding, not an assertion about every file.
* The [Melanie project](https://hurlburt.faculty.unlv.edu/mitwebsite/) supplies another original interview route. Only its index was screened in this update. Third-party DES summaries were used as locators, not as verbatim participant data or trusted form labels. The AI DES-interviewer preprint [10.48550/arXiv.2607.20310](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.20310) describes a developing method; it is not treated here as an already validated full-model dataset.

## Why a full fit is still not defined

A real test needs a declared observation relationship, for example `y_i(t) = H(S_i, u_i, A_i, history_i) + measurement error`. **H is presently unspecified**, not an equation already supplied by the corpus. The data can contain imperfect or indirect observations; it is not necessary to observe every field component or know a person's entire life. But we must state what an observed report constrains, and represent missing history and inputs with an explicit uncertainty model.

There are specific mathematical reasons not to fit arbitrary numbers now:

1. The velocity force depends on the magnitude of the S gradient. Simultaneously reversing signed S and excess utility can preserve velocity while reversing S and A, as the full coupled tests already demonstrate. A movement-only score cannot select between those states. This is a mathematical sign ambiguity, not a claim that opposing human meanings are identical.
2. For nonzero gamma, any differentiable proposed A can be made compatible with the accumulator by choosing `U = U_baseline + A'/gamma`. Allowing unconstrained missing U to absorb every observation would therefore not test the accumulator's predictive value.
3. Under the implemented normalized source and periodic zero-flux domain, the spatial mean obeys `d(mean S)/dt = kappa A`. That is an exact consequence used for conservation checks, not permission to identify a mood or confidence rating with mean S.
4. The present runner records Smoke/Clay history attribution, but those labels alone do not change the dynamics. Separate role effects require observation functions or additional coupling assumptions. Keeping a history ledger is not evidence that a distinct role mechanism has already been tested.

The remaining observation questions are concrete: how to quantify the expressed idea and the inward response, how reports constrain spatial S and u, what measures utility, how each form is recognized without forcing it, and how timing and missing prehistory are handled. Water's perceived balance, Mist's unspoken agreement, Wind's felt time, Fog's retrieval clarity and D2O's small-change/large-consequence comparison have not all been measured together in the inspected records.

**The search found useful component evidence, not a dataset currently sufficient for the requested full predictive test.** This is neither a rejection nor a validation of the full theory. No synthetic person was assembled by joining different studies. No empirical full-model score, model-selection holdout result, causal history effect or new Navier–Stokes law is claimed. The earlier eight reserved NEMAR participants remain untouched for response analysis.

## Reproduce

Use Python with `pypdf` and `openpyxl`. From this directory:

```text
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python fetch.py --raw /path/to/local/research-records
python audit.py --raw /path/to/local/research-records --check
python verify.py
```

[sources.json](sources.json) records all URLs, hashes and byte sizes. Downloads are only public records. Original transcripts and the workbook are kept outside this publication; their publicly visible status is not treated as permission to redistribute entire personal narratives or copyrighted sources. [The plan](../PRIMARY_RECORD_PLAN.md) was saved before downloading the transcript PDFs, after paper and dataset-card inspection; it is not a registered or untouched protocol. [Results](results.json) and [annotations](annotations.json) distinguish automated indexing, close reading, reports, interpretation and unmeasured quantities.
