The mathematical source is the user's attached corrected corpus. Its filename, SHA256 and selected paragraphs are retained in `corpus_source.json` and `corpus_extract.json`. The original stays local. **DeliData:** Georgi Karadzhov, Tom Stafford and Andreas Vlachos (2023), *DeliData: A Dataset for Deliberation in Multi-party Problem Solving*, PACM HCI 7(CSCW2), article 265, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3610056). [Author manuscript](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/6de14ac6-3604-4651-88d0-69311c9c6b1b/content). The article identifies the vowel/odd rule and discusses a Creative Commons 4.0 corpus release. Cornell says the original distribution's license applies; we do not infer broader redistribution rights from the software license. The [Cornell documentation](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/deli.html) describes the conversion. No original dialogue text is published here.

Downloaded archive: [Cornell deli-corpus.zip](https://zissou.infosci.cornell.edu/convokit/datasets/deli-corpus/deli-corpus.zip), 939,850 bytes, SHA256 `ad24289c4d75593e22fb640fec55186dbb6a10b2cba61d7f854aba04df8c208d`. The analysis uses actual SUBMIT events and original_text, not approximate tracked beliefs. Its one INITIAL event and incomplete card vocabularies are documented limitations. The published paper's aggregate gain is not represented as reproduced by our different observed-transition cohort.

**Interpretive connection:** René Girard's *Things Hidden Since the Foundation of the World* motivates the question about doubles. The rival-double concept is not an observed variable in these corpora. Helpful uptake is this project's operational description. No quantitative parameter or causal result is attributed to Girard. The book's oxygen/mimesis comparison discussed earlier is an explanatory analogy, not a molecular mechanism.

Results are newly computed here; they are not numerical findings copied from the papers. The corpora are historical records. No people were contacted, no new moderation interventions were performed, and no language-model service was used to manufacture participant conversations. The assistant's functional review is clearly identified as interpretation of existing text.
