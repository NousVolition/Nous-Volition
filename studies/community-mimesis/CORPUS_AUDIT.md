The supplied corrected corpus is the specification for this extension. Its file hash and paragraph numbering are in [corpus_source.json](corpus_source.json); relevant paragraphs are preserved verbatim in [corpus_extract.json](corpus_extract.json). There are 624 nonempty body paragraphs and no embedded equation objects or images; the equations are Unicode text. The original DOCX has not been edited.

**Earlier work used additional models, not an exact implementation of this corpus throughout.** The community allocation model optimizes assumed skills, needs and work budgets. The encounter model uses a logistic restriction probability and `memory_next = retention × memory + gain × restriction`. Its own methods label these choices synthetic. Those are explicit modeling additions, but they cannot be presented as a numerical test of your accumulated-source law. The public-record studies compute actual status comparisons and block-duration statistics; they do not solve your field equations. This audit does not say those computations were fabricated or invalid. It corrects their relationship to your specification.

The checked earlier files are `community-power/model.py`, `community-encounters/model.py`, `community-encounters/METHODS.md`, `community-record-tests/compare_wording.py` and `community-record-tests/analyze_history.py`. ## What was actually solved

The scalar transport verification specifies length 2π, periodic boundaries, constant divergence-free velocity 0.4, diffusion 0.05, initial field sin(x), and normalized smooth source kernel proportional to exp(3 cos(x−π)). These are **declared numerical fixtures**, not fitted social coefficients. Units can be taken as an abstract length and time system; no metres, seconds, viscosity or pressure are attributed to people. Gamma, kappa and constant excess input are 1 for the source check. Consequently A(t)=t and the integral of S increases by t²/2. The no-source control compares with exp(−0.05t)sin(x−0.4t).

The forcing and stress functions are evaluated and tested, but a fully coupled momentum/pressure/S system is not solved here. The user-supplied system still needs its domain, boundary and initial data, coupling and observation mapping for that empirical use. The exact-edge optimizer likewise needs a measured U and metric; inventing these would be the substitution this audit is designed to avoid.

The additional phrase and answer retrieval procedures are in [observations.py](observations.py), explicitly labelled measurement procedures. They test observable questions suggested by the corpus. They are not replacements for its dynamics and do not make a similarity score into Sens, geometric distance, or force.

## Corrections to the community guide

The corrected corpus supplies an explicit **field accumulation law**. What remains unspecified is a law connecting actual penalties to that field and subsequent W decisions. Saying simply that memory has no equation would be inaccurate. The earlier logistic and decaying-memory simulation remains available as a labelled extension. Its 25.4-point wording effect and later penalty effects must not be attributed to the corrected corpus equations.

Your language stays intact: **preserves Sens while adjusting Sin**, Socio-Didact, boundary, pressure, force, refusal, resistance, exclusion and adaptation retain their local meaning and evidential status. Water, Fog, Smoke or Clay can describe a person's situation without becoming a fixed identity or a score of human worth.
