# Public-record wording and penalty tests

Fixed before retrieving or inspecting this iteration's outcomes, 2026-10-21 local date. Exploratory follow-up, not an external preregistration. Earlier Reddit and Wikipedia findings are known. Publish positive, negative and inconclusive results, without turning association into causation.

## Wording and recorded treatment

Use OFAI's One Million Posts v1.0.0 archive of German-language DER STANDARD comments (2015-2016). Use recorded online/deleted status, not the later annotation labels. First search within the same article, same author and same parent (including both top-level) with creation times within 24 hours. Exclude newspaper staff. Combine headline and body after HTML decoding and whitespace normalization; retain case and punctuation in the audit. Both full texts must contain at least five word tokens and 30 characters. Exact normalized texts are a separate consistency control.

Changed-text candidate search: character SequenceMatcher ratio >= 0.85 and token-set Jaccard >= 0.75, with length ratio >= 0.70. The screen is a retrieval method, not a meaning judgment. Construct candidates without reading moderation outcomes. Greedily retain non-overlapping post pairs by decreasing similarity then ascending IDs. Sort by SHA256 of 'wording-v1|earlier_id|later_id' and audit up to 40 changed pairs. Audit at most 40 exact-text controls by the same hash order. Record overall coverage and counts for all eligible exact controls separately.

Review changed pairs with statuses hidden. Include title, parent and article title for referents. Assess proposition, target, stance, negation, quantities, accusation and pragmatic force, including insults or requests. Strict-equivalence pass requires no material difference on these dimensions. Allow uncertain; do not force a binary match. Record whether the change is lexical or only typography/formatting. A single assistant reviews German texts; no independent human raters or verified speaker intentions. Save decisions before joining recorded outcomes. Outcomes do not revise the semantic decisions.

For strict-equivalence pairs and rejected/uncertain pairs separately, count online/online, online/deleted, deleted/online and deleted/deleted in earlier/later order. Exact duplicates supply a control for differing treatment without a wording change. Report within-hour and within-10-minute sensitivity, and the corpus's available parent/article coverage. These selected repeated posts may be moderated for repetition or other context; same author and parent do not equal identical time, moderator, reports or policy exposure. Recorded statuses lack decision timestamps: never infer that the earlier post was already deleted when the next was submitted, or that posts are revisions of one another. A contrast cannot establish a wording-caused effect.

## Prior penalties and later recorded decisions

Extend the already reconstructed public Wikipedia blocks using the same 180-day cohort and episode rules. Distinct block episodes begin after the preceding episode ended; reblocks within an active episode are modifications. Require first observed episode from 2006 onward, finite initial assignment, a known first episode end, full 180-day follow-up, no reconstruction issues, and a distinct second block within that window. Preserve initial indefinite second assignments in coverage; finite pairs enter duration comparisons. This is a selected recurrently blocked population, not all encounters or a random sample of editors.

Retrieve the two initial decision comments by log ID from the original public source. Compare finite first/second assignments in: all pairs; exact normalized nonempty same reason; same moderator; both same reason and same moderator. Reason normalization only HTML-decodes, normalizes whitespace, and case-folds. Do not strip evidence, dates, user mentions or links to make reasons match. Exclude any missing moderator from same-moderator comparisons. Preserve 30-day and 90-day gap sensitivities within the fixed 180-day complete-follow-up cohort. Do not relabel reason equality as conduct equality.

Report n, longer/equal/shorter, medians of initial assigned hours, median log2 duration ratio, paired bootstrap interval (2,000 whole-account draws, seed 20261021), and exact two-sided sign test. As a predeclared description of duration dependence, report Spearman correlation of first and second log-duration in the exact-reason and both-controls groups. No account identifiers, raw comments or sanction narratives published. These controls reduce some observed differences but leave conduct severity, selection, reporting, policy drift and history visibility unresolved. There is no exogenous variation in prior punishment, so causal institutional or psychological memory is not identified.

## Reproducibility and provenance

Pin downloaded source hashes. Preserve source data locally and publish aggregate results, semantic audit reasons with nonidentifying pair hashes, code, figures, and an inspection command for authorized local reproduction. OFAI data: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Schabus, Skowron and Trapp (SIGIR 2017); no raw corpus redistribution here. Wikipedia log provenance and source episode code are retained from the previous study. Tests must cover strict text-prefix selection independent of outcomes, semantic-decision locking, duplicate controls, time windows, episode continuity, reason normalization, accounting, paired statistics, and deterministic reruns. Existing synthetic community parameters will not be fitted to incompatible endpoints.
