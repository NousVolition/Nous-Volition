# Source records, attribution and publication scope

The **One Million Posts Corpus v1.0.0** is by Dieter Schabus, Marcin Skowron and Martin Trapp, with DER STANDARD. Cite *One Million Posts: A Data Set of German Online Discussions*, SIGIR 2017, DOI [10.1145/3077136.3080711](https://doi.org/10.1145/3077136.3080711). The [authors' site](https://ofai.github.io/million-post-corpus/) and [repository](https://github.com/OFAI/million-post-corpus) provide the data under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Corpus-derived review and aggregate material in this study is shared under those same terms. Analysis, selection, semantic judgments and any errors in this extension are ours; source authors do not endorse them.

The OFAI archive and extracted SQLite hashes are in sources.json. The source spans June 2015 through May 2016. The original schema describes recorded online/deleted status separately from annotations. We do not infer a moderator identity, decision timestamp, reason or policy from that field.

The **Wikipedia public block log** is the Cornell ConvoKit public release linked in sources.json and the [dataset catalog](https://convokit.cornell.edu/datasets.html), associated with Jonathan P. Chang and Cristian Danescu-Niculescu-Mizil, *Trajectories of Blocked Community Members: Redemption, Recidivism and Departure* (2019), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.08628). This study uses the previously downloaded byte-pinned release and does not claim it is a current complete Wikipedia history. We do not assign a new license to those underlying records; fetch and inspect them at the source.

No raw comments, user/account names, source post/log IDs or individual sanction narratives are included in the publication. Published pair hashes support reconstruction from the licensed corpus; they are lookup keys, not a guarantee that the underlying posts could never be located. Local source files, pools, account hashes and caches stay outside the publication directory. The duration histogram contains only counts for each pair of assigned lengths and declared analysis subgroup; it allows the published statistics to be reproduced without account records.

The publication includes scripts, frozen review reasons, aggregate counts, plots and reports. Existing repository terms govern original code where applicable; this note does not replace a source license or authorize redistribution of source corpora.
