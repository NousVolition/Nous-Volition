## Question and permissible inference

Preserve the fixed force direction, original gradient magnitude, and each of the three distinct response laws. Do not insert decay, logistic decisions, or a penalty feedback equation. P545 supplies stress. The corpus supplies no observation equation tying S or U to task correctness. The following mapping is an explicitly proposed, falsifiable observation hypothesis, not an equation attributed to the corpus.

**Candidate mapping H:** within a group/participant's successive recorded card submissions, U is the binary objective correctness of the most recent observed submission; the next observed correctness is compared to the spatial integral of S on a unit-area domain. Time advances by one per submission interval, not by measured seconds. During each interval the input is held at the preceding observed correctness. A(0)=0; the initial integral of S equals the first observed correctness. No intermediate field or memory reset, no clipping of the signed field to [0,1], and no use of the next response as its own predictor. This is an observation-driven retrospective one-step comparison, not a fully autonomous multi-step forecast. Correctness is not measured intended meaning, personal utility, agreement, or moderation.
