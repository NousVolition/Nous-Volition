TWO ADDED METHODS: PARTICLE CAPTURE AND WATER-INTERFACE TOPOLOGY
Streams and Rocks / substance interactions — added 2026-10-15 local date

These additions answer two different questions in the same study:
  1. Does an airborne particle reach and remain on a falling drop?
  2. What hydrogen-bond network surrounds a water molecule at the interface?
The first predicts transport; the second measures molecular structure.
No untested conversion between these quantities is introduced.

1. PARTICLE CAPTURE: THE BEARD ROUTE

The passage supplied by the user points to Beard (1974), not the separate
Beard (1976) terminal-speed parameterization already used in our model.
Beard (1974) numerically calculated axisymmetric steady flow around rigid
spheres up to Reynolds number 400, then particle trajectories including
gravity, Stokes–Cunningham resistance and electrical forces. The experimental
drop diameters were 0.40–0.85 mm, with 0.4 micrometre-radius particles.
Rear/wake capture is physically relevant in that study. This supports a
resolved-flow and trajectory approach, but does not establish its validity
for deformable drops at Re about 1466–2576 in our latest comparison.

Mechanism                 Meaning and current implementation
Brownian diffusion        Random thermal displacement. Included as a
                          collection correlation in the frozen model.
Direct interception       A finite-sized particle touches the drop even
                          when its center approximately follows the air.
                          Included as a correlation; not an attractive force.
Inertial impaction        Particle inertia prevents following diverted air.
                          Included as a corrected collection correlation.
Diffusiophoresis           Drift from a gas-composition/vapor gradient.
                          Included as signed approximate surface flux.
Thermophoresis             Drift driven by a temperature gradient.
                          Included with the same surface-flux approximation.
Electrophoresis            Electric-field drift of charged particles.
                          NOT included in the neutral frozen model.
Wake capture              Included as a correlation only for 20<=Re_D<=800;
                          unresolved outside that interval, not absent in nature.
Retention/coalescence     Perfect sticking assumed; rebound and film rupture
                          are not calculated. Encounter is not retention.

We use 'direct interception' for the user's 'directional interception'.
We separate 'diffusiophoresis' from 'electrophoresis' rather than treating
'diffusion electrophoresis' as one universal mechanism. Neither dominance
nor direction follows from drop size alone: gradients, charge, flow, particle
size and material also matter. The neutral control has no electric force.

A future resolved calculation must specify liquid properties and interface
shape, solve the air/liquid flow with appropriate boundary conditions, and
transport particles using resolved drag and specified forces. Brownian
motion requires a stochastic term when relevant. Finite-particle contact
and an independently justified retention rule are separate decisions.
Efficiency E must be measured from incident particle flux and declared
reference area. Mesh, time-step, domain-size and sampling convergence, plus
an untouched matched experiment, are needed. A steady rigid-sphere solution
at Re<=400 cannot simply be extrapolated to a deforming high-Re wake.

2. WATER-INTERFACE TOPOLOGY: IMPLEMENTED AND CHECKED

Jedlovszky, Dellago and Sega (2025) apply Foffi and Sciortino's descriptor
psi to interfacial water. Molecules form an undirected hydrogen-bond graph.
An edge requires O--O <3.5 A and donor-H--acceptor-O <2.5 A, in either donor
direction. No angular criterion is added here. For molecule i, find all j
whose shortest bond path has exactly FOUR links; psi_i is their minimum
oxygen--oxygen distance under the minimum-image convention. Classify
psi<5 A as HDL-like and psi>=5 A as LDL-like. This is not the fourth-closest
spatial neighbor, a fourth direct bond, or any arbitrary four-step walk.
The published TIP4P/2005 slab study used ITIM layers with a 1.5 A probe.

Our new module is interface_topology/water_topology.py. The supplied
five-water chain is an artificial calculation check, not equilibrated
water. It produces four bonds; only its two ends have fourth-shell
neighbors, both at psi=11.2 A. The remaining three are UNCLASSIFIED.
Missing neighbors are stored as JSON null, never silently as LDL-like.
The saved results and 14 passing tests are in interface_topology/.
Five seeded random graphs additionally agree with an independent
Floyd–Warshall implementation for all 175 molecule/shell comparisons.
Those seeds test graph code; they are not repeated molecular simulations.

The tests explicitly show why cutting a network is dangerous: removing
a shortcut node can change a shortest path from two to four bonds without
moving the retained molecules. That can change psi or make it newly defined.
Compute the graph on the complete supplied system before selecting layers.
Our summary reports classification coverage and HDL fraction among the
classified molecules; absent values are not put in the denominator.

Input requires oxygen and two hydrogen coordinates PER water molecule in
angstroms (1 nm = 10 A). Orthorhombic periodic boxes and mixed periodic
directions are supported. Triclinic boxes are rejected. External layer
labels may be supplied, e.g. independently obtained ITIM labels, but this
module does not identify interfaces or implement ITIM. It makes O(N^2)
geometric comparisons per snapshot and is a reference implementation,
not an optimized production trajectory analyzer. No uncertainty estimate
can be obtained from the artificial single snapshot.

HDL-like and LDL-like are structural labels within a specified water model.
They do not, by themselves, prove two coexisting phases. A 5 A threshold is
not automatically transferable to other potentials, clay, smoke or mixtures.
Before comparing real interface populations, supply equilibrated slab/drop
trajectories, a documented force field and interface definition, sufficient
independent samples, and block uncertainties. Audit threshold/H-bond choices,
finite size and graph truncation. The paper's interfacial populations have
NOT been reproduced here; our completed result is algorithm verification.

HOW THESE TWO COULD BE CONNECTED

psi can describe a simulated water interface. To affect a capture prediction,
one must separately measure or compute how that interface changes a quantity
the transport model uses, such as sticking probability or surface tension,
under matched conditions. That proposed relationship must then be tested
against data not used to select it. No psi-to-capture coefficient, invented
force, chemical reaction, or personal/community classification is inferred.
The frozen raindrop source and its old outcomes are unchanged.
